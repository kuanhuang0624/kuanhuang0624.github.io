Our research focuses on artificial intelligence, computer vision, medical imaging, and emerging
quantum machine learning methods. We develop practical, trustworthy, and efficient AI methods for
biomedical and healthcare applications.

### Research Areas

- Medical Image Analysis
- Breast Cancer Detection and Risk Prediction
- Medical Video and Vision-Language Modeling
- Trustworthy and Efficient AI
- Quantum Machine Learning

### Current Research Projects

1. Breast Cancer Risk Prediction from Longitudinal Mammograms
2. Weakly Supervised Breast Ultrasound Analysis
3. Medical Vision-Language Models and Report Generation
4. Trustworthy, Efficient, and Quantum AI for Medical Imaging

### Research Opportunities

Students interested in joining our research projects are welcome to contact me. Undergraduate
students should have completed or be currently taking a Data Structures course. Graduate students
have no specific course prerequisites. All students should have strong coding skills,
self-motivation, and a genuine willingness to learn.

Prospective M.S. and Ph.D. students may email their CV and a brief description of their research
interests to [khuang@kean.edu](mailto:khuang@kean.edu).
