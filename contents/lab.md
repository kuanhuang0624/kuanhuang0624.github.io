My research group is based in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Kean
University. We work on machine learning for medical imaging, with an emphasis on methods that
learn from limited or weak annotation and that quantify their own uncertainty. Undergraduate
and graduate students contribute to the work through independent study, the CAHSI Local
Research Experiences for Undergraduates (LREU) program, and the Students Partnering with
Faculty (SpF) program.

### Research Areas

- Breast ultrasound segmentation, classification, and BI-RADS categorization
- Weakly supervised, semi-supervised, and active learning for medical images
- Uncertainty quantification, fairness, and robustness of diagnostic models
- Multi-task learning, attention and transformer architectures for medical imaging
- Large language models and multimodal methods for radiology

### Current Projects

1. **Breast cancer image analysis** (2016 – present) — segmentation and classification of
   breast ultrasound, mammography, and multi-modality images.
2. **Chest X-ray image analysis** (2023 – present) — weakly supervised localization, large
   language models for medical image analysis, and radiology report generation.
