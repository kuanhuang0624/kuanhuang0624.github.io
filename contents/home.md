<div class="home-bio">

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Technology
at Kean University (Union, NJ). Before joining Kean, I was a post-doctoral research fellow at
the Lester and Sue Smith Breast Center and the Department of Molecular and Human Genetics at
Baylor College of Medicine (Houston, TX). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from
Utah State University (Logan, UT) in 2021 and my B.Eng. from Harbin Institute of Technology
(Harbin, China) in 2016.

My research interests are artificial intelligence, computer vision, machine learning, deep
learning, pattern recognition, and medical image analysis, with a particular focus on
label-efficient and trustworthy methods for medical imaging.
